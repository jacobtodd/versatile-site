A songwriting notebook that helps you find the right word.

Write in verses and choruses, tap a line's last word for rhymes, and watch your rhyme scheme take shape as you write, on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Find the rhyme

Tap the last word of any line for perfect rhymes and near rhymes.

See the scheme

Each rhyme gets a letter and a colour in the margin, so you can see where a verse holds together and where it drifts.

Find the meaning

Check what a word really says without leaving the page.

Write in sections

Verses, choruses, bridges and hooks, plus the key, tempo, bar counts and credits for every song.

Bring your lyrics

Paste from Notes, or import text, Word, RTF and PDF files. Versatile spots the verses and choruses for you.

Share and print

Send a clean PDF lyric sheet, print it, or export a whole notebook with its contents page.

Every device

Notebooks sync through your own iCloud account. No sign-up, no separate account.

Real notebooks

Leather binder, composition book or botanical cover, with page turns and a bookmark you place yourself.
